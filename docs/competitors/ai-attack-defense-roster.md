## Services — the lane that is actually open

| Firm | Type | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Astra Security, Bishop Fox, NetSPI, Praetorian, Trail of Bits, NCC Group | Established pentest + AI add-on | Broad enterprise base. AI testing often bolted onto generic pentest, not deep specialization |
| AI Red Teamers, Redbot Security | Boutique, human-led AI pentest | US-based senior engineers, no outsourcing (self-claim). Scale-limited, custom quote only |
| HackerOne, Bugcrowd, ActiveFence freelancers | Individual | $200–$100,000 per finding |

The established firms are wide but shallow on AI. The boutiques are deep but capacity-capped. That is the seam.
