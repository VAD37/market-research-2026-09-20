## The one honest answer

**Nothing in this table mandates third-party AI red-teaming or security testing as of 2026-09-17.** Every regime that touches AI in Southeast Asia does so through **data-protection impact-assessment and governance-disclosure obligations**, not a testing requirement — the same shape already found for the EU AI Act and NIST/ISO in `../../../docs/markets/ai-attack-defense.md` and `../../../docs/markets/ai-attack-defense-services.md`. The closest thing to a purchase trigger in this table is **Indonesia's DPIA requirement for automated decision-making with legal consequences**, because a DPIA is a documented risk assessment a company might commission help for — but it is a privacy-impact assessment, not an AI red-team engagement, and nothing in the sourcing found says a DPIA must include adversarial testing.

This matches, rather than contradicts, `../../../docs/raw/services-lane-pulls.md`'s Finding 2 for the US/EU: **auditor and underwriter discretion, not law, is the actual demand engine** ("auditors are now demanding AI-specific evidence in real engagements... red-team suites... prompt-injection test artifacts"). No equivalent auditor-discretion evidence was found for Southeast Asia specifically in this pass — that is an open question, not a negative finding, since it was not directly searched.

## What this means for "find the trigger, not the fear" (the brief's own instruction)

On the evidence gathered: there is no SEA-specific regulatory trigger strong enough to force a purchase today. A SEA-facing buyer's actual reasons to pay would have to come from one of: (a) a foreign counterparty's own compliance requirement flowing downstream (e.g., a US/EU customer's vendor-security questionnaire, per `../../../docs/raw/services-lane-pulls.md` Finding 2's "vendor security questionnaires... whether products have undergone independent AI red teaming"), (b) Indonesia's DPIA obligation if the buyer is Indonesia-based and doing automated decisioning, or (c) plain reputational/incident-avoidance motivation with no regulatory backing at all. None of these is confirmed as an active, working sales motion for AI security testing specifically in this region — that remains untested.
