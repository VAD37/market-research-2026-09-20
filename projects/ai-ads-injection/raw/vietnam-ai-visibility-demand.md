## 1. Vietnam/Vietnamese-user AI-assistant usage figures

### Decision Lab survey (the one Vietnam-specific usage figure found)

Source: Báo Pháp Luật Việt Nam (`doanhnhan.baophapluat.vn`), reporting a Decision Lab survey.
URL: `https://doanhnhan.baophapluat.vn/bao-cao-decision-lab-80-nguoi-viet-dung-ai-chatgpt-thong-tri-nhung-ai-noi-gay-bat-ngo-ve-do-hai-long-85891.html`
Published: **2025-08-25**. Survey fieldwork: **2025-07-20 to 2025-07-28**.
Kind: third-party-reported (press write-up of a market-research firm's survey; not the Decision Lab primary report itself, which was not located this pass).

Verbatim (Vietnamese) + translation:

- "gần 80% người dùng trực tuyến tại Việt Nam đã sử dụng AI trong 3 tháng qua" — *"nearly 80% of online users in Vietnam used AI in the past 3 months."*
- Platform share among Vietnamese AI users: ChatGPT **"81% người dùng lựa chọn"** (chosen by 81% of users), Gemini **"51%"**, Meta AI **"36%"**, AI Hay (Vietnamese-made assistant) **"9%"**, Kiki (Vietnamese-made assistant) **"3%"**.
- "trung bình khoảng 2 nền tảng AI khác nhau" — *"users use an average of about 2 different AI platforms."*
- "một nửa số người dùng trả phí đăng ký" [sử dụng AI để kiếm tiền] — *"half of paid-subscription users [use AI to earn money]"* (exact scope of this clause not further disambiguated by the fetch).
- AI Hay ranked second in customer satisfaction, after ChatGPT and ahead of Gemini.

**Caveat carried forward: this survey is ~13 months old relative to the 2026-09-17 pull date and its sample size/methodology beyond the fieldwork dates was not stated in the write-up.** The Decision Lab primary report was not located/fetched directly this pass — this is a press paraphrase of it, one hop removed from the source.

**This article carries no Vietnam-specific breakdown — global web-traffic share only.** Included here only because it is the freshest (2026-01) data point surfaced by a Vietnamese-language search, and because it is written up by a Vietnamese outlet (relevant to what Vietnamese business press is telling local readers about the AI-assistant landscape, even though the underlying number is global).

## 2. Vietnamese digital-marketing agencies selling GEO / AI-search-visibility services — confirmed, multiple agencies

Search surfaced at least 8 distinct Vietnamese agencies branding this service under the name "GEO" (Generative Engine Optimization) or "AEO" (Answer Engine Optimization), explicitly targeting ChatGPT/Gemini/AI Overview visibility: TopOnSeek (TOS), Hashmeta Vietnam, Nextop Digital Agency, SEODO, seo.danang.vn, Fast Marketing, Midas Agency. This directly contradicts any assumption that "GEO" is not yet a locally sold service category in Vietnam — **it is, under that exact branded name, as of 2026-09-17.**

## Caveats

- Vietnam-specific AI-assistant user-count/penetration figure: not found. The only Vietnam-tied usage figure is the Decision Lab survey (2025-08-25 write-up, fieldwork 2025-07), which is 13+ months stale relative to this pull and one hop from the primary source.
- The Similarweb/Vietstock figure (2026-01-28) is global market share, not Vietnam-specific — included for context only, flagged clearly above, must not be read as a Vietnam number.
- Pricing was pulled from four agencies' own marketing pages, not from a signed contract or an independent price survey — normal vendor sales-page inflation/anchoring risk applies, same as any vendor-reported price elsewhere in this project.
- No agency's page stated a specific "answer-integrity" or "defense against injected content" line item — all four sell visibility/appearance optimization (get the brand mentioned), not audit or integrity verification. This is the same distinction the project's existing `docs/customers/answer-integrity-buyer.md` draws for the English-language market: visibility vendors are common, integrity/audit vendors are not. That pattern appears to hold in the Vietnamese market too, on this evidence, though only 8 agencies were sampled and none was checked for an integrity-specific offering beyond its GEO/AEO landing page.
- Decision Lab's primary report was not located or fetched; only a press paraphrase was read.
